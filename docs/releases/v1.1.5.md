# Nexus 1.1.5

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Uploaded prints are real prints now.** Prints you upload in Inventory used to show up in VRChat as a plain photo. They now come out like the in-game camera's: a white print card with your caption, "Captured By" and the date.
- **Your whole picture fits.** The image is fitted into the print's photo window without cropping. Pictures that aren't 16:9 get white margins.
- **See it before you upload.** The upload window shows the exact print that will be uploaded and updates as you type the caption.
- **Prints saved from VRChat upload as they are.** A finished print you saved from VRChat is uploaded unchanged instead of getting a second frame.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
