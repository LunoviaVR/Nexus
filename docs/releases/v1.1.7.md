# Nexus 1.1.7

## ✨ New

- **You can see when VRChat is rate limiting you.** A yellow "Rate limited" notice pops up in the corner whenever VRChat says you're going too fast, so you know why something didn't load or didn't go through.
- **It tells you whether to wait.** If something you did was turned away, the notice asks you to wait a moment before trying again. If only Nexus' background loading was slowed down, it says so, and that loading carries on by itself.
- **One notice, not a pile of them.** A burst of rate limits keeps the same notice up instead of stacking new ones, and it replaces the red error you'd otherwise get for an action that was rate limited.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
