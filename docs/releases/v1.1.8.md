# Nexus 1.1.8

## 🛡️ Fixes

- **Your history is safer.** If Windows Credential Manager wasn't ready when Nexus started (say, right after you log in to Windows), Nexus could replace the key that unlocks your history and lose everything it had recorded. Now it stops and tries again on the next start instead.
- **Your VRChat login stays with VRChat.** Saving an image now only downloads from VRChat's own servers, so your session can never be sent to a look-alike address.
- **No more doubled notifications.** Signing out or switching accounts while your friends list was still loading could leave the old live connection running, so every alert showed up twice until you restarted Nexus.
- **Friend changes are recorded reliably.** New friends and unfriends could be missed for a while when Nexus was busy reading a large game log.
- **VRChat's settings file is written safely.** Changing your photo folder can no longer leave VRChat's config half-written if your PC crashes at that moment.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
