# Nexus 1.1.0

## 🛠️ Improved

- **Your theme and accent color stick.** Nexus now saves them in its own encrypted database the moment you pick them, so they're still there after you quit Nexus, restart your PC or shut it down. Before, they could be lost if Nexus closed soon after you changed them.
- **Accent colors in rainbow order.** **Settings → Appearance → Accent color** now goes **Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet**, and hovering a color shows its name. If you'd picked a color that's no longer in the list, Nexus switches you to the closest one.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
