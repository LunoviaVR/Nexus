# Nexus 1.1.4

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Friends stay up to date without pressing refresh.** Who's online or offline, where friends are and what they change now keep updating on their own, including after your PC sleeps or your network drops.
- **Nexus notices when its live connection has died.** The connection to VRChat could stop working without any sign of it, still showing as connected while nothing new came in. Nexus now checks on it regularly and reconnects within about a minute.
- **Missed updates get caught.** Nexus compares your friends list with VRChat every 5 minutes in the background, and again when you come back to the window after a couple of minutes away. Anything VRChat didn't send live still shows up, and goes in your Feed.
- **Open profiles update as you watch.** Bio and link changes appear on a profile that's already open, and its Activity tab keeps up with new Feed entries.
- **Quick profile edits aren't missed.** If a friend edits their bio right after Nexus last looked at it, Nexus now looks again a few minutes later instead of up to 30 minutes later.
- **Waking your PC doesn't flood you with alerts.** When a catch-up finds a lot of online and offline changes at once, they go in your Feed without a desktop alert for each one.
- **Fewer surprise sign-outs.** If the live connection is refused because your session ended, Nexus signs you back in with your saved login instead of sending you to the sign-in screen.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
