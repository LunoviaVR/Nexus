# Nexus 1.0.5

## ✨ New

- **Show what you're playing on Discord.** Turn on **Settings → Discord → Show what you're playing** and your Discord profile shows the world you're in, with its picture and creator, the instance type and region, how many people are there, and how long you've been in it. It follows you from world to world and clears a few seconds after you quit VRChat. It's off until you turn it on, and it needs the Discord desktop app.
  - **Hide private instances** (on by default): in Invite, Invite+ and group-only instances, Discord only says you're in a private instance.
  - **Join button** (off by default): lets people open your instance in VRChat straight from your Discord profile. It's never shown for instances that need an invite. Discord doesn't show your own buttons to you, so ask a friend to check it.
  - The switch shows what Discord is displaying right now, or why it isn't, for example when Discord isn't running.

Nexus passes your activity to the Discord app on your PC, and Discord shows it on your profile. Nothing else is shared, and nothing leaves your PC while the switch is off.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
