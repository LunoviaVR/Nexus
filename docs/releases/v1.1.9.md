# Nexus 1.1.9

## 🛡️ Fixes

- **Player counts are right again.** A friend's profile, and the world it opens, could show 0 players in the instance they were in, even after refreshing. Nexus now uses the best count VRChat gives, never fewer than the friends it knows are there, and the live count when you're in the same instance.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
