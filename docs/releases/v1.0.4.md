# Nexus 1.0.4

## ✨ Improved

- **See more from a world's creator.** In a world's details, the creator's name ("by …") is now a link. Click it to open their profile straight on the **Worlds** tab, with all of their public worlds to search and sort. Close the profile to go back to the world.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
