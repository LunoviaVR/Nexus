# Nexus 1.0.1

A quick fix release.

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Avatars page crashing with "This panel hit an error" (`unityPackages?.some is not a function`).** For some avatars, VRChat sends platform information in a different format than usual, and a single one of them could take down the whole Avatars page. Nexus now copes with those avatars, and the same protection covers avatar details, group tags and the platform filter on Group Instances.

- **"Recent" sorting did nothing on the Friends page.** At startup every friend was stamped "seen just now", so they all tied and fell back to alphabetical order. Offline friends now sort by when they were last seen, and online friends by when they arrived, with VRChat's own last-activity time breaking ties. The friends panel and a profile's Mutuals tab sort the same way.
- **Switching off the Favorites filter hid your favorites.** Your favorite friends now move back into In game or Active on web instead of disappearing.
- **Searching looked broken when matches were in a switched-off section.** The Friends page now says so and offers **Show all sections**.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
