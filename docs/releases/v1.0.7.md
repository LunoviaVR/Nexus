# Nexus 1.0.7

## ✨ New

- **Pictures for everyone on the Players page.** People who aren't your friends now show their profile picture instead of a letter, along with their trust rank, and so do you. Nexus remembers anyone it has already seen (your friends list, profiles you opened, searches, group member lists) and quietly looks up the rest in the background, so in a busy instance the pictures fill in over a few seconds.
- **Pictures in the Game Log.** Open a past session and everyone who was there gets their picture too, not only your friends. Very old log entries didn't record who someone was, so those still show a letter.

## 🛠️ Improved

- **Smoother around VRChat's limits.** Nexus now paces everything it asks VRChat for, so it stays quick without getting your account slowed down:
  - What you click (invites, boops, edits, favorites) always goes first, then whatever you're looking at. Nexus' own background work, like bio checks, pictures and world names, only uses the room that's left.
  - Quick bursts, like opening a profile, go straight through.
  - If VRChat asks Nexus to slow down, everything pauses for as long as VRChat asks, then carries on a little slower for a few minutes. Instead of a spinner that never ends, you see how long to wait, for example *Try again in about 10 seconds*.
  - Pictures load on their own track, so a page full of them never holds anything else up. A picture that didn't load tries again a little later.
- **Live updates don't stall.** Friend updates no longer wait on looking up a world's name, and a shaky connection no longer reloads your whole friends list every few seconds.
- **Group member search** waits until you stop typing instead of searching on every letter.
- **"Most online" group sort** loads its counts in the background, so you can keep browsing while it fills in.

## 🛠️ Fixed

- **Staying signed in.** If VRChat was busy or limiting requests when Nexus started, Nexus could sign you out. Now it only asks you to sign in again when your session has really ended.
- **Platform filters in Discover.** Turning a platform off now hides every avatar that has a version for it, instead of only taking its tag off the card. For example, turn off **Quest** and **iOS** to see PC-only avatars.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
