# Nexus 1.0.9

## ✨ New

- **Start an instance from any world.** Open a world and click **New instance** next to its list of instances. Choose who can join:
  - **Public**, **Friends+**, **Friends**, **Invite+** or **Invite**.
  - **Group**, for an instance owned by one of your groups. Nexus lists only the groups whose role lets you open instances, with the ones you own first. Then you set the same things as in a group's Owner tools: members only, Group+ or Group Public, limiting it to certain roles, a queue, and 18+.

  It opens in the region you're in now, and you can pick another. Your new instance shows at the top of the world's list, highlighted, with **Join** and **Invite me** right there. Next time, Nexus starts on the type and group you used last.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
