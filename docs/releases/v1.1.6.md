# Nexus 1.1.6

## 🛠️ Improved

- **Nexus feels faster.** What you click and open now goes to VRChat straight away instead of waiting its turn behind other requests, so profiles, worlds and groups open without pauses.
- **Rate limits don't freeze the app.** If VRChat rate limits something you did, you're told straight away. Before, everything in Nexus waited until the limit lifted.
- **Refresh whenever you like.** The refresh buttons on profiles, worlds, groups, the friends list and Group Instances no longer make you wait 30 to 60 seconds between refreshes. A new refresh can't start while one is still running.
- **Your friends list loads faster.** Nexus now fetches several pages of it at once instead of one after another.
- **Names and pictures show up sooner.** Players in your instance and in the Game Log are looked up more quickly.
- **Still gentle on your account.** Loading the friends list and Nexus' own background lookups (players, bio checks, prints) are still paced so your account isn't rate limited, and they try again by themselves if VRChat asks them to slow down.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
