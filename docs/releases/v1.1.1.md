# Nexus 1.1.1

## 🛠️ Improved

- **Gentler on VRChat's servers.** When several parts of Nexus ask VRChat for the same thing at the same moment, it's now fetched once and shared. You'll run into VRChat's "too many requests" limit less often.
- **No knocking on closed doors.** When VRChat says a world, user, group or instance doesn't exist or is private, Nexus remembers that for 15 minutes instead of asking again. Joining a group, accepting a friend or making any other change clears that memory, so you never see an outdated answer.
- **Expired sessions are handled cleanly.** When your VRChat session expires, Nexus stops sending requests and signs you back in with your saved login. If you didn't save your login, it asks you to sign in once, instead of letting every page fail one at a time.
- **Group instances are checked less often.** The open group-instance list now refreshes every 5 minutes instead of every 2. If VRChat asks Nexus to slow down, it keeps showing the last list and tries again 2 minutes later, instead of showing an error.
- **Smoother live updates.** Reconnecting to VRChat's live feed starts at a calmer pace, and the catch-up after a reconnect happens quietly in the background. Duplicate events VRChat sometimes sends are now ignored.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
