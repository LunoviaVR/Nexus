# Nexus 1.2.0

## ✨ Improvements

- **Your instance at a glance on Home.** The "You're in" card now shows friends and everyone else side by side, like the Players page. You see who just arrived, their status or trust rank, and how long they've been there, with a link to the full list.

## 🛡️ Fixes

- **Profile pictures show up in the "You're in" card.** Only friends had pictures there, so you and everyone else showed an empty circle.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
