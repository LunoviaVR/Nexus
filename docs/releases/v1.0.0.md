# Nexus 1.0.0

The first public release of **Nexus**, a fast, modern VRChat companion for Windows. It keeps track of your friends, the worlds you visit and the people you meet, and keeps that history private on your PC.

> **Download:** `Nexus_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe` below (recommended) or the `.msi`.
> Windows 10/11, 64-bit. From here on, Nexus updates itself.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- **Live friends list.** Status and location changes arrive the moment they happen over VRChat's live connection. Friends are grouped by the instance they're in.
- **Your whole VRChat history, searchable.** A feed of everything your friends did, plus a game log of every world you visited, who was there and what played.
- **Profiles made for Nexus.** Banners that fade into the page, at-a-glance stats about your time together, and one click from any encounter to that session in the Game Log.
- **Private by default.** Everything Nexus stores is **encrypted on your PC**, with the key kept in Windows Credential Manager.
- **Coming from VRCX?** Import your history in one click and pick up right where you left off.

---

## 👥 Friends & people

- Live friends list with **Favorites, In game, Active on web and Offline** sections, search by name, status or world, and A–Z or Recent sorting.
- Hover a friend to **Join**, **Invite**, or **Request invite** without opening anything.
- **Discover** people who aren't your friends yet and send friend requests from the same dialog.
- **Friend alerts:** ring the bell on any friend's profile to be notified when they come online or go offline. Manage everyone you've belled in *Settings → Notifications → Friend alerts*.
- A friends panel on the right of every page, grouped by instance or status, with your favorites on top.

## 🪪 Profiles

- A fully redesigned profile with a full-width banner hero. When someone has a banner, the whole page takes on its colours with a soft fade.
- Stats at a glance: **time together, encounters, last together and friends since**. On your own profile: play time, sessions, VRChat Credits and join date.
- **Overview** with where they are right now (as a world card you can click), bio and links, your VRChat **note**, and a private **memo** that never leaves your PC.
- Tabs for **Groups, Mutuals** (friends and groups), **Worlds, Avatars** (what you've seen them wearing), **Activity** and the raw **JSON**.
- **Activity → Recent encounters:** click one to jump straight to that session in the Game Log, expanded and highlighted.
- Actions: Join, Invite me, Invite, Request invite, **Boop** (with emoji), Favorite, Add friend.
- **Edit your own profile:** picture, bio, pronouns, links, languages, icon frame, nameplate effect, profile effect, showcased badges and represented group. Set your status from the account menu in the top bar. Your account settings (avatar cloning, booping, mobile invites, mutuals and Discord visibility) toggle right from your profile.

## 🌍 Worlds, groups & avatars

- **Worlds:** browse popular, recent, favorite and your own worlds, search VRChat, and open any world to see its instances, friends inside, and Join or self-invite.
- **Groups:** all your groups with search and sorting by name, members or members online. **Discover** groups to join or request to join. The sidebar shows how many groups you're in.
- **Group Instances:** active instances across all your groups with friends-first sorting and filters for access, region, platform, full and age-gated. The sidebar shows how many are active right now.
- **Avatars:** switch to your own avatars or favorites, and discover public avatars by name, author or tags (via the community index [avtrDB](https://avtrdb.com)).

## 📜 Feed, Game Log & Players

- **Feed:** online/offline, location, status, avatar, bio and friend/unfriend changes, all filterable and searchable.
- **Game Log:** read straight from VRChat's own log files, so it works even when the API doesn't. Every world visit becomes a session with a **neat player list** (time each person was there, rejoins merged) and a **timeline** of joins, leaves and videos.
- **Players:** everyone in your current instance, friends first, updating live as people come and go, plus a "Recently left" list.
- Nexus notices when you **quit or crash VRChat**, so Home and Players never show you in a world you've already left.

## 🔔 Notifications

- Invites, invite requests, friend requests and **boops** in one place behind the bell in the top bar, which shows the unread count.
- **Filter by type** (All, Invites, Invite requests, Friend requests, Boops).
- Accept friend requests, join invites, send invites back, **Boop back**, or dismiss.
- Windows notifications with **separate switches** for friends coming online, going offline and joining your instance, plus invites, invite requests, boops, friend requests, new friends and unfriends.

## 📊 Insights

- Hours played per day, a **when-you-play heatmap**, your top worlds and the friends you spend the most time with.
- Ranges: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, Year and **All time**.

## 🖼️ Inventory

- Your **prints, emojis, icons and banners**, with upload (VRC+), set-as-icon, full-size viewing, download and delete.

## 🔒 Privacy & security

- **Encrypted at rest.** Your history, memos and settings live in an **SQLCipher-encrypted** database. Cached images are encrypted with **AES-256-GCM**, under keyed file names that don't reveal what you looked at.
- The encryption key is **random, generated on your PC** and stored in **Windows Credential Manager**. Other apps, and anyone browsing your files, only see scrambled data.
- **Upgrading from a pre-release build?** Your existing data is encrypted automatically on first launch, and the old unencrypted image cache is deleted.
- Want your data in the clear? **Settings → Privacy → Export decrypted copy** saves a readable SQLite file, deliberately and only where you choose.
- Your VRChat session and (only if you tick *Remember password*) your password are stored in Windows Credential Manager, never in plain files.
- Nexus talks only to VRChat (plus GitHub to check for updates, and avtrDB when you use Avatar discover). No analytics, no telemetry.

## 🔁 Moving from VRCX

- **Settings → Data → Import from VRCX** finds VRCX's database automatically and brings over your friend feed, game log (worlds, players, videos), "friends since" dates and memos.
- Only history from before Nexus started recording is imported, so nothing is doubled, and running it again is safe. VRCX's own files are never modified.

## ⚡ Quality of life

- **Ctrl + K** command palette to jump to any friend, world, group or page.
- Dark and light themes with an accent colour picker; stays usable down to small window sizes.
- Tray icon with a status switcher, close-to-tray, and start with Windows (minimized).
- Multiple VRChat accounts with quick switching.
- CSV export of your feed, game log, worlds and friends.
- **Automatic updates** from GitHub Releases, signed and verified, installed with one click.

## Known limitations

- Windows only for now.
- VRC+ uploads (icons, emojis, prints, banners) need an active VRC+ subscription, as in VRChat.
- The Game Log and Players page need VRChat's logging enabled (it is by default).
