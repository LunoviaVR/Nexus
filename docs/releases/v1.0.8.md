# Nexus 1.0.8

## ✨ New

- **Owned groups.** The Groups page and every profile's Groups tab have an **Owned** filter, and groups someone owns get an **Owner** badge. Profiles also list the groups that person owns right on their overview. VRChat only shares the groups people show publicly on their profile, so a group someone keeps hidden won't appear.
- **Run your groups from Nexus.** Open a group you own and the new **Owner tools** let you:
  - **Edit the group:** name, description, rules, links, languages, who can join, and its icon and banner. Pick them from your VRC+ icons and gallery, or upload a new one right there.
  - **Post announcements** to members or to everyone, only to certain roles if you like, with or without a notification. Old posts can be deleted.
  - **Open group instances** in any world (recent, favorites, your own, a search, or a pasted world link) with the access, region, queue and 18+ settings you want, and **close** them, optionally sending everyone inside home.
  - **Answer join requests:** accept, decline, or decline and block. The Members tab shows how many people are waiting.
  - **Manage members:** see each member's roles and everything those roles allow, give or take roles, and kick or ban people. Banned people are listed with an **Unban** button.
- **Moderators get the parts they're allowed to use.** If your role in someone else's group can, say, manage posts or bans, those tools show up for you too.
- **Mute, hide, block and unfriend from a profile.** The new **More** button on anyone's profile lets you mute their voice or chatbox, hide their avatar, block them, or unfriend them. It all applies in VRChat too: VRChat loads these settings when it starts, so restart it if it's open. Hidden avatars are saved straight into VRChat's own settings on your PC, the same place VRChat keeps them. Their profile shows what you've set (for example **Voice muted** or **Blocked**), and unfriending or blocking asks you first. When you unfriend or block someone yourself, Nexus no longer pops up a "no longer your friend" alert about it.

## 🛠️ Improved

- Kicking, banning or accepting someone updates the group's member count right away.

## 🛠️ Fixed

- Group owners no longer see a **Leave** button on their own group, which VRChat doesn't allow.

## ⬆️ Updating

Nexus offers this update automatically. Click **Install & restart** when the notification appears, or go to **Settings → Updates → Check now**.
