# Nexus: brag plan

**What it is:** A free, tiny (~6 MB) Windows companion app for VRChat. It shows where your friends are live, lets you join them in one click, and keeps a private, encrypted history of every world and every encounter.
**For:** VRChat players who'd otherwise keep the VRChat website open in a browser tab.
**What sets it apart:** It's live (straight off VRChat's connection), it remembers (Game Log from VRChat's own log files), and it never leaves your PC (no servers, encrypted).
**Hook:** The question every VRChat player asks: *"Where is everyone right now?"*, answered by live friend cards popping in.
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle
"Your VRChat friends, live on your desktop." (the site's own headline). Lead with the live feeling, prove it with the real app, then close with what's reassuring: private, tiny, free.

## Tone
`default`: punchy, playful, clean. Soft transitions, dip through the dark navy background between busy layouts.

## Visual identity (from website/styles.css)
- Background `#080b12` with indigo `#6b70ee` / cyan `#1da6da` aurora orbs and film grain
- Gradient text `#cfd2ff → #8d94f5 → #2bc0ee`
- Inter (variable) for type, JetBrains Mono for small labels
- Real assets: `website/assets/icon.png`, app screenshots from `website/assets/screens/` in the site's `.device` window frame, the site's floating "live" cards and status dots
- Every name in the screenshots comes from Nexus's built-in demo account (Aurora, Nova, The Black Cat…), so they're already fictional. No real users appear.

## Sound
About 118 bpm. A soft synth pad and a round sub bass in A minor (Am–F–C–G), with a gentle pluck arpeggio. Card pops, the Join click and the checkmarks are short plucks tuned to the chord tones, mixed under the music. Low-passed noise swells sit under each scene change, and the music resolves to A on the logo.
